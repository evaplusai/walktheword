Walk the Word · Design 01 · Wireframes · Edition 1 scope

The word, with its doors drawn on.

Eight screens for a Bible that is walkable, graded, warranted, versioned, and bound to lived testimony. The page you read stays a page — a whole book, in order, in a book face on paper. The graph never performs; it waits underneath as quiet dotted doors. Every pin below ties a pixel to the requirement in the PRD that put it there.

The grade system — explicitness, never truth AStated. The text says it in words. BTextual, unstated. Parallels, shared detail. CTradition. Outside the text — always hollow.
Product palette

Paper, ink, and the three grades. No brand color competes with scripture; the only saturated hues on screen carry meaning.

Type roles
Scripture is set in a book face.

Old-style serif for the text itself, quiet grotesque for chrome, monospace only for references and edition metadata — the “receipt” voice.

The one interaction

A dotted underline is the entire affordance vocabulary: every door looks like this. Tap → card → walk. No icons, no badges in the text column, no engagement chrome anywhere.

  2. 2The book is the frame. You are always inside Matthew, not inside “the graph.” Chapter position replaces any feed or home screen. PRD §6.5 books are the foundation layer

Screen 02

Connection card — the receipt, then the walk

GradedWarranted

Tapping “the prophecy of Esaias” lifts a card: grade, the target passage in scripture’s own words, the exact warrant verse, and one action.

MatthewCh 13 · v 14

14And in them is fulfilled the prophecy of Esaias, which saith, By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and shall not perceive…

AMatthew 13:14 quotes Isaiah 6:91
Isaiah 6:9 · KJV

And he said, Go, and tell this people, Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not.2

Warrant: Matthew 13:14 — “in them is fulfilled the prophecy of Esaias, which saith…”3

  1. 1The claim is named, not interpreted. “Matthew quotes Isaiah” — an assertion of the text about the text. Never “Isaiah predicts…”. PRD §6.1 assert, never adjudicate
  2. 2Scripture answers scripture. The target is the verse itself, verbatim — no summary, no AI paraphrase, ever. PRD §6.2 no generated content
  3. 3The warrant is a link, not a footnote. The exact verse that creates this edge, one tap for the skeptic. PRD §4.3 · §10 every claim traceable
  4. 4“Walk” is the product’s verb. You land in Isaiah 6 in its own book context, and can keep walking from there. PRD §4.1 walkable

Edition 1What changed
  1. 1Silence is a first-class answer, set in the scripture face with a grade-A rule — the product’s most trust-building moment. PRD §5 “says plainly when the text does not address”
  2. 2Everything below is quotation with a chapter-and-verse receipt. Retrieval, ranked and cited — composition is architecturally impossible. PRD §6.2 · §10 traceable by a skeptic
  3. 3Open questions stay open, honestly. The hollow C chip carries the tradition layer; the changelog tracks if evidence moves. PRD §4.4 · §11 non-converging questions
